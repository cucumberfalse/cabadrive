# Cabadrive

## Problem

An experienced Russian-speaking driver in Buenos Aires (CABA) can fail the local theory exam not because of driving skill, but because of Spanish wording, local legal differences, and administrative exam topics.

## Solution

Cabadrive is a local-first web trainer focused on CABA theory exam preparation. It keeps official Spanish question text primary, adds clearly marked unofficial Russian learning support, and supports offline study after build.

## Key Value

- Exam-focused, not a full driving school or full Spanish course.
- Structured around official-source validation and traceability.
- Optimized for users who already drive and need fast adaptation to CABA-specific exam realities.

## High-Level User Flow

1. User opens the local app and picks a mode: study, exam simulation, mistake review, vocabulary, or CABA-vs-RF differences.
2. User studies official questions with optional Russian translation, explanation, and term hints.
3. User runs exam practice, reviews weak topics, and iterates until stable passing performance.

## Target Audience

Primary users are Russian-speaking drivers with practical experience and low Spanish proficiency (A1 or below) who need fast, practical preparation for the CABA theoretical exam.
