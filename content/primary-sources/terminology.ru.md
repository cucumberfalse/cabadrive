# Russian Primary-Source Terminology Guide

Status: published guidance for Cabadrive learner-facing Russian translations.

This guide governs Russian full translations and simple Russian rewrites under `content/primary-sources/`. It does not change the Spanish official archive. When a term has legal force, preserve meaning and scope before improving style.

## Core Rules

- Keep the official Spanish source as the authority. Russian text is unofficial learning support.
- In full translations, keep legal structure visible: article numbers, conditions, exceptions, penalties, competent authorities, dates, deadlines, and document names.
- In simple Russian, explain what the learner should understand, but do not soften duties, bans, penalties, deadlines, or exceptions.
- Do not invent simplified Spanish. Spanish terms may appear only as source labels or quoted official names when needed.
- Prefer consistent terms across chunks even when the Spanish source varies slightly.
- If a Spanish term is ambiguous in context, keep the narrower legal meaning and add a short note rather than guessing.

## Numbers, Dates, Citations, And Penalties

- Preserve all numbers exactly unless the target language requires grammatical words around them.
- Keep units and legal thresholds: km/h, metros, días, años, unidades fijas, categories, vehicle weights, ages, and blood-alcohol values.
- Dates should keep the source date and may use Russian month names only when it does not obscure the official format. Do not change time zones or effective dates.
- Article references must remain traceable: `artículo 5`, `art. 5`, `inciso a)`, `Anexo L`, and law/decree numbers must survive in both full and simple Russian.
- Penalties must keep the type, trigger, range, duration, authority, and exception. Do not summarize `fine plus disqualification` as only `punishment`.
- Exceptions and conditions must stay attached to the duty they limit. Use `кроме`, `если`, `при условии`, `за исключением`, and `когда` explicitly.
- When a source says an authority `may` act, do not translate it as `must`. When it says `must`, do not translate it as advice.

## Cabadrive Disclaimer Wording

Use this trust boundary consistently near learner-facing source content:

```text
Неофициальный учебный слой Cabadrive. Официальным источником остается испанский текст из архива.
```

Simple Russian may explain the same idea as:

```text
Это учебное объяснение Cabadrive на русском. Юридически главным остается официальный испанский текст.
```

Do not imply that Cabadrive translations are legal advice, certified translations, or replacements for the Spanish official documents.

## QA Expectations

- QA notes should mention this guide when terminology choices were checked.
- If a term intentionally diverges from the preferred rendering, record the reason in QA notes or process memory.
- For document-specific recurring terms, add a short note to the shard QA rather than editing the official archive.
